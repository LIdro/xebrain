# Xebrain Manifesto

**Xebrain is a founder's state of mind restoration system, not a project manager.**

A founder moves between ideas, decisions, doubts, conversations, and responsibilities. An interruption costs more than time: it can break the connection to why something mattered, what was being considered, and where the thinking was about to go.

Xebrain exists to make that connection recoverable. Its purpose is to help a founder return to a line of thought with enough context to continue it honestly.

## Continuity of thought over productivity

The central measure of value is whether the founder can recover and continue their thinking. More completed tasks, more activity, and more reminders do not establish that value.

An unfinished question can be worth preserving. A doubt can be essential context. A discarded option can explain a decision. The system must make room for ambiguity instead of forcing every thought into an actionable task.

Restoration means recovering the founder's working context: intent, reasoning, assumptions, uncertainty, and the perspective they were using. It does not mean diagnosing or treating a psychological state.

## The loop: capture -> understand -> preserve -> re-enter

### Capture

Let the founder put down a thought before it disappears. Accept fragments, questions, decisions, and rough notes without requiring a project, deadline, or complete explanation. Keep the original words and the moment of capture.

### Understand

Help make the thought's context explicit: what prompted it, why it matters, what is believed, what is uncertain, and how it relates to earlier thinking. Any interpretation must remain distinguishable from the founder's own words and open to correction. Understanding must not invent certainty or intent.

### Preserve

Keep the thought together with the context needed to return to it. Preserve its original expression, relevant role, connected thoughts, decision reasoning, open questions, and subsequent changes. A later conclusion must not erase the uncertainty that preceded it.

### Re-enter

Restore an intelligible starting point: where the founder left off, why it mattered, what remained unresolved, and what has changed since. Offer a way to continue the thought, not a demand to complete a task. New thinking feeds back into capture and begins the loop again.

## Temporal re-entry

Temporal re-entry is returning to a line of thought after time has passed: an hour, a week, or months. A chronological list alone is insufficient. The founder needs the state of the thinking at departure and a clear distinction between that earlier context and later developments.

For example, returning to a pricing question should restore the alternatives considered, the assumptions behind them, the unresolved concern, and any newer evidence. It should help the founder recognize their earlier reasoning without treating it as permanently true.

## Role re-entry

Role re-entry is recovering the perspective needed when moving between responsibilities: strategist, builder, seller, operator, or another role the founder chooses. These are lenses on thought, not job titles or task assignments.

The same subject can have different meaning in different roles. A customer request may raise a trust question for the seller, a feasibility question for the builder, and a positioning question for the strategist. Xebrain should preserve those perspectives and their connections so that switching roles does not require rebuilding context from memory.

Temporal and role re-entry work together. A founder may return to an old customer conversation specifically as a strategist and need both its history and the relevant perspective restored.

## Product commitments

- Preserve the founder's voice and agency. The founder can correct interpretations, revisit decisions, and choose what to continue.
- Keep source material available alongside summaries. A synthesis is a route back to thought, not a replacement for it.
- Preserve uncertainty and changes in reasoning. Distinguish what was known then from what is known now.
- Make capture forgiving and re-entry calm. Neither requires an inbox to be cleared or a productivity score to improve.
- Treat thoughts, context, and their continuity as the core objects. Projects may supply context, but must not become the organizing purpose of the product.
- Evaluate every proposed feature by how it supports capture, understanding, preservation, or re-entry.

## Boundaries

Xebrain must not drift into a generic project management tool. Task boards, deadline enforcement, team assignments, sprint planning, completion streaks, and productivity scoring are outside the initial product direction.

If a founder records a possible next step, preserve it as part of their thinking. Do not automatically turn it into an obligation.

The promise is simple: **help me find my way back into what I was thinking, why it mattered, and where I can continue.**
